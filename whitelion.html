How installation works

  1. Site visit

    We walk the home with you or your architect and note every light, curtain, fan and appliance you want to control.

  2. Plan and quote

    You get a room-wise plan: which panel goes where, what each button does, and the scenes we will set up.

  3. Installation

    Whitelion-trained technicians fit the panels in place of your switches. Modular and retrofit designs fit existing boxes in most homes.

  4. Set-up and handover

    We pair every device to the AirHome Pro app, create your scenes, and show the family how to use them.
