Installation, by ceiling.

The controller sits in the fan's top cup, so designer fans use only the rod and kit supplied with them, never a pre-installed rod.

False or gypsum ceiling

A 6 × 6 inch box in 19 mm ply is fixed to the concrete ceiling above. The false ceiling is built to touch its base, and the fan screws into the box, which carries the full weight.

Wooden ceiling

Fans fix directly to a wooden false ceiling of at least 12 mm plywood, strong enough to take the weight of the fan.

Concrete ceiling

Fans fix directly. If there is a pre-installed fan hook or fan box, a fabricated clamp may be needed; we supply it at extra cost.
